Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

EchoFlux ("we", "our", "us") is an all-in-one creator platform that includes AI content tools, social media planning, and Fan Hub — customizable storefronts where creators can connect with their audience.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

We collect your name, email address, and payment information when you register. For creators using Fan Hub, we also collect information required for Stripe Connect to process payouts.

Media Content

We collect images, videos, and audio files you upload, including content in My Vault, profile pictures, Fan Hub posts, and media attached to products.

Fan Hub Data

For creators: storefront settings, products, posts, and member interactions. For fans/members: subscription and purchase history, and messages sent within the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Operate and provide the features of EchoFlux
  • Process payments and payouts through Stripe
  • Display your content on your Fan Hub storefront
  • Provide AI-generated content suggestions
  • Send service-related notices
  • Monitor and improve the service

3. Data Sent to AI Models

To provide AI features, prompts and uploaded media may be sent to third-party AI providers (such as Google Gemini) to generate captions, strategies, and other content.

We do not use your data to train our own AI models. Third-party providers process data under their own policies.

4. Fan Hub — Creator and Member Data

Creators: Your storefront is public. Display name, bio, avatar, and public content are visible to visitors. Your email, payout details, and analytics are private.

Fans/Members: When you subscribe to a creator, they can see your display name, email, and purchase history with them. Creators cannot see your activity with other creators.

Messages: Direct messages are stored securely and accessible only to participants.

5. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Stripe. Payment card details are handled directly by Stripe and not stored on our servers. See Stripe's privacy policy.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers (Stripe, Firebase, AI providers) as needed to operate the service, and as required by law.

7. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud) with industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate measures to protect your data, but no method is completely secure.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account data by contacting us. Visit our Data Deletion page for more information.

9. Children

EchoFlux is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Updates will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
