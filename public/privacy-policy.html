Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

This policy explains how EchoFlux.ai and witme.io collect and use personal information for creator and fan features.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details (email, username/display name, identifiers)
  • Purchase and billing metadata

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide services, process transactions, deliver paid access, send notices, detect abuse/fraud, and improve performance.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Card details are processed by Stripe and not stored directly by us. See stripe.com/privacy.

4. Data Visibility on Creator Pages

Creators can see fan information needed to deliver offerings on their own page. Creators do not get cross-creator purchase history.

5. Sharing and Providers

We do not sell personal information for money. We share data with hosting, security, analytics, and payment providers as needed, and when required by law.

6. Cookies and Security

We use cookies/local storage for login, preferences, analytics, and abuse prevention. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

7. Retention and Rights

We keep data as needed for service operations, compliance, and dispute handling. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, or portability.

Use our Data Deletion page or contact support to exercise rights.

8. Minors and Updates

Our services are not intended for minors. We may update this policy and will update the date above when changes are posted.
