# Creator OS

Creator OS helps creators turn attention into a simple daily money workflow.

The page is built around one question:

**What should I do today?**

## How It Works

1. **Build My Money Flow**
   - Choose your weekly goal, audience, content lanes, posting rhythm, monetization paths, and brand tone.
   - Creator OS saves this as your working profile.

2. **Get Today’s Move**
   - Creator OS turns your setup into a specific daily plan:
     - what to post publicly
     - what hook and caption to use
     - what to mention in Stories
     - what Amazon category or link to test
     - what to drop inside Inner Circle

3. **Publish Through the Bridge**
   - `Send to Create Post` moves the caption, hook, and context into Create Post.
   - Create Post is where Instagram publishing happens because Instagram requires attached media.
   - Once the creator adds/selects media, the existing Instagram publisher can publish now or schedule the post.
   - `Post to My Page` publishes the idea directly into the creator's public/member page as a Fan Hub drop.

4. **Move Ideas Through the Flow**
   - Save posts as content ideas.
   - Track each idea through:
     - Ideas
     - To Film
     - Ready to Post
     - Posted
     - Monetized
     - Review

5. **Plan the Week**
   - Generate a simple 7-day plan.
   - Each day has a public post, Story/Amazon link, and Inner Circle drop.
   - Edit anything manually.

6. **Store Amazon Links**
   - Add product links, categories, audience fit, and content situations.
   - Products can be owned, curated, or just tested for interest.

7. **Find Product Trends**
   - Pro and Elite users can run Amazon product trend searches.
   - Trend Finds are converted into creator actions, not raw search results.
   - A trend can become a content idea or a saved Amazon link.

8. **Use the Inner Circle Funnel**
   - Edit the welcome post, first 48-hour plan, weekly retention plan, and Treat upsell ideas.

## How It Ties Together

- **Creator OS** is the workflow brain. It decides what deserves attention today and tracks each idea through money stages.
- **What to Post** is the idea generator. It can still create raw public post ideas when the creator wants fresh options.
- **Create Post** is the composer and social publisher. Creator OS sends Instagram-ready captions there, then Create Post handles media, publish now, and scheduled Instagram posting.
- **My Page / Fan Hub** is the owned audience destination. Creator OS can post directly there as a free drop, subscriber drop, or locked Treat as the flow expands.

## Access

- Free users see a locked preview.
- Pro, Elite, Agency, and Admin users can use Creator OS.
- Instagram publishing uses the existing connected Meta/Instagram permissions through Create Post.
- My Page publishing writes directly to the creator's Fan Hub feed.

## Important Principle

Creator OS is planning-first, but it is connected to publishing.

It helps the creator decide what to make, where it goes, and how it connects to money. My Page can be published directly from Creator OS. Instagram flows through Create Post because Instagram requires media and uses the existing connected account publisher.
